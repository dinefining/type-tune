Info
Type Tune is a tool to convert text to music.

Click anywhere to create new text. Click to edit or move it around by dragging.
Hit play or space to convert text to tune.
Click and draw over an area to select multiple items.
Copy and Paste duplicate the selection. Or hold Alt/Opt and drag.
Tap the bin, press Delete, or drag text onto the bin to delete it.
Drag the loop lines to set the playback range.
Top: scan direction (left), how it sounds in the middle (sound, scale, chord / arpeggio / melody, note length), help (right). Bottom: copy and paste (left), tempo, play and text size (middle), delete (right).
Tempo and size open sliders: drag, or tap the number to type one.

A Maybe Machine by Ravi Popat.
